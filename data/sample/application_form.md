# Dayton Area Literacy Collaborative — 2027 Family Reading Grant (FICTIONAL SAMPLE)

*This application form is invented for testing.*

The Dayton Area Literacy Collaborative supports nonprofit organisations that
help children in kindergarten through third grade, and their families, build
strong reading habits in Montgomery County, Ohio.

**Award:** $20,000 to $60,000 for one year.
**Deadline:** Applications are due January 15, 2027 at 5:00 PM Eastern.
**Eligibility:** 501(c)(3) organisations serving Montgomery County with an
annual operating budget under $3 million.
**Matching funds:** Not required.
**Reporting:** A mid-year progress update and a final report with outcome data.

## Application questions

1. Organisation background: Briefly describe your organisation's mission and history. (200 words maximum)
2. Statement of need: What literacy need will this project address, and for which children and families? (300 words maximum)
3. Project description: Describe the activities you will deliver with this grant, including how families will be involved. (400 words maximum)
4. Outcomes and evaluation: What results do you expect, and how will you measure them? (250 words maximum)
5. Sustainability: How will this work continue after the grant period ends? (1,000 characters maximum)

## Attachments

- Project budget
- Most recent audited financial statements
- List of board members
