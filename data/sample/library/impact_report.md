# 2025-26 Impact Report

## Who we serve
- 612 students tutored at 9 partner schools.
- 88% of students qualify for free or reduced-price lunch.
- 71% of students are Black, 14% Hispanic/Latino, 11% white, 4% multiracial.
- 19% of students are English learners.

## Outcomes
- 68% of tutored students who started the year below benchmark on the DIBELS 8 assessment reached benchmark by spring, compared with 41% of similar non-tutored students at the same schools.
- Average gain of 1.4 grade levels in oral reading fluency.
- 93% of parents surveyed said their child reads more at home.

## How we measure
Student assessment results are tracked in a shared database updated three times a year (fall, winter, spring) using DIBELS 8.
