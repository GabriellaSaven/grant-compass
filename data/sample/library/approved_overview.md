# Organisation Overview (approved language)

Riverbend Reads helps Dayton children in kindergarten through third grade become confident, grade-level readers. Since 2014 we have paired trained volunteer tutors with students who are behind in reading, meeting twice a week during the school day at partner elementary schools. We also equip families with simple at-home reading routines and send every child home with books for the summer. In 2025-26, 68% of tutored students who started the year below benchmark reached it by spring.
