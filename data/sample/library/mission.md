# Riverbend Reads — Mission and Theory of Change

Riverbend Reads, Inc. is a 501(c)(3) public charity incorporated in Ohio in 2014 and based in Dayton, Ohio. Programs run in Montgomery County, Ohio.

## Mission
Riverbend Reads helps children in grades K-3 from low-income families read at grade level by the end of third grade.

## Theory of change
If children who are behind in reading receive consistent one-on-one tutoring from a trained adult, and their families have simple routines for reading at home, then they will reach grade-level reading by the end of third grade. Reading at grade level by third grade is a strong predictor of later school success.
