# Programme Descriptions

## One-on-One Tutoring
Trained volunteer tutors meet each student twice a week for 30 minutes during the school day at 9 partner elementary schools in Dayton Public Schools. Tutors complete 6 hours of training in structured literacy before they begin. Served 612 students in the 2025-26 school year.

## Family Reading Nights
Monthly evening events at partner schools where parents learn simple at-home reading routines. 1,050 family members attended in 2025-26.

## Summer Book Bags
Each enrolled student takes home 10 free books for the summer. 6,100 books distributed in summer 2025.

## Plans
- Expand tutoring to 3 more Dayton schools in 2027-28 (about 200 more students).
- Pilot a math tutoring program; no curriculum chosen yet.
