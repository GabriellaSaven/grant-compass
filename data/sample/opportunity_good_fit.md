# Miami Valley Early Learning Fund — 2027 Community Grants (FICTIONAL SAMPLE)

*This opportunity is invented for testing.*

The Miami Valley Early Learning Fund invites proposals from nonprofit
organizations working to improve early literacy outcomes for children in
Montgomery, Greene and Miami counties, Ohio.

## Priorities
- Programs that help children in kindergarten through third grade reach grade
  level reading.
- Work that engages parents and caregivers as partners in learning.
- Organizations that measure results with a recognized assessment and share
  data with the Fund.
- Strong preference for programs serving children from low-income households.

## Eligibility
- Applicants must be a 501(c)(3) public charity.
- Applicants must serve residents of Montgomery, Greene or Miami County.
- Annual operating budget between $250,000 and $5 million.
- Organizations must have completed an independent audit or financial review
  within the last two years.

## Award
Grants of $50,000 to $100,000 per year for up to two years.

## Deadline
Full proposals due December 4, 2026 at 5:00 PM Eastern.

## Application requirements
- Narrative (max 8 pages): need, program design, outcomes and evaluation plan.
- Program budget and budget justification.
- Most recent audit.
- Board list with affiliations.
- Two letters of support from school or community partners.
