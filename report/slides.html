Grant Compass

An AI development team for small nonprofits: it finds grants that fit, tells you honestly whether to apply, and helps you write answers that are true to your own records.

Gabriella Saven · github.com/GabriellaSaven/grant-compass

The problem

I was a fundraiser. Most of my week went to paperwork.

Searching funder sites to check eligibility. Rebuilding last year's answers for new word limits. Checking every number before it went out. Then reports, then the board update.

Small nonprofits don't lose grants because of bad programmes. They lose them because one or two people can't find the time.

Cost cuts

  • Code filters results before any AI call
  • Pages re-read only when they change
  • Cache-friendly prompts: 88% of input cached
  • Small model, low reasoning, hard caps

Did they hurt quality? (18-run test)

  • Caching: 35% cheaper, same prompt, no quality cost
  • Medium reasoning: +58% cost, slower, decisions no more consistent
  • So: keep it small, and flag borderline calls

Safety

13 of 13 attack and failure tests passed

  • Prompt injection in funder pages, documents and answers: ignored, flagged in red, scores computed in code
  • Invented facts: quotes must match word for word; the Director's numbers must match real metrics
  • Wrong actions: the chat assistant's tools are read-only
  • Data leaks: one database per organisation; links can't reach private networks
  • Overspending and loops: 6 calls, 120k tokens per run; size limits before any AI call

Reproduce: python tests/safety_tests.py (about $0.002)
