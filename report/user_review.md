# User review: a friend with grant-fundraising experience, after trying Grant Compass

Received 25 October 2026, verbatim.

> Gabi I just had a proper play around with Grant Compass and I’m honestly so impressed. It actually feels like something I would use in real life, which I wasn’t expecting from a student project.
>
> The grant discovery and fit assessment are really useful because it gets to the part that takes so much time in fundraising, which is working out whether something is actually worth pursuing before you sink hours into an application. I also really liked that it doesn’t just give you a random score, it explains the reasoning and flags concerns.
>
> The pipeline is probably my favourite bit. Being able to see deadlines, risk, funding amount, where each application is at and what the next action is all in one place is genuinely helpful. The writing side is also really strong. The Grant Coach feels much more useful than just asking ChatGPT to rewrite something because it actually tells you what is missing, whether you’ve answered the question properly and where the evidence is weak.
>
> The whole thing feels very intuitive and much more joined up than most grant tools I’ve used. I could genuinely see this saving a lot of time for a small fundraising team, especially where one person is juggling research, deadlines and writing at the same time.
>
> My only thought would be to keep pushing the organisation knowledge/evidence side because that is where I think it becomes especially valuable. But overall, I loved it. It feels like it was designed by someone who actually understands how grant fundraising works rather than just adding AI onto a generic CRM.
